# Crisis Response Templates

## IMMEDIATE PUBLIC RESPONSE (Google/Yelp — 1-star, unanswered)

Use within 4 hours of detecting a crisis-level review.

```
[Reviewer's first name], I'm [your name], the owner of [Business Name].
I'm sorry this was your experience — it's not acceptable and not who we are.
Please call me personally at [phone] or email [email] so I can make this right.
```

---

## GENERAL 1-STAR RESPONSE

```
[Name], thank you for taking the time to share this. I'm genuinely sorry we
let you down — this isn't the standard we hold ourselves to. I'd love to make
it right. Please reach out at [contact info] so we can talk directly.
```

---

## 1-STAR RESPONSE (Factual Dispute — Stay Neutral)

Never argue facts publicly. Instead:

```
Thank you for the feedback. We take every experience seriously and would like
to understand more about what happened. Please contact us at [phone/email] —
we want to make sure every customer is heard and helped.
```

---

## MEDIA / JOURNALIST INQUIRY

If a journalist contacts the business regarding a reputation issue:

```
Thank you for reaching out. [Business Name] is committed to [relevant value —
e.g., customer safety, quality service, our community]. We are [investigating /
aware of / addressing] the situation and will provide a full statement by [date].
In the meantime, please contact [name] at [email/phone] for official inquiries.
```

---

## SOCIAL MEDIA STATEMENT (Facebook/Instagram)

For when a negative post about the business goes viral on social:

```
We're aware of the concerns being shared about [Business Name] and take them
seriously. [One sentence acknowledging the issue without admitting specific fault.]
We are [taking action / investigating / have resolved] this situation. Our
commitment to [customers / quality / safety] is unwavering. If you've had a
negative experience, please contact us directly at [contact] — we want to
make it right personally.

— [Owner name], [Business Name]
```

---

## STAFF BRIEFING POINTS

What to tell staff so they respond consistently if customers ask in person:

1. "We're aware of the feedback and are addressing it directly."
2. "If you have specific concerns, please speak with [manager name]."
3. "We take every customer experience seriously and are working to improve."
4. Do NOT: discuss specifics of any complaint with other customers.
5. Do NOT: share opinions about negative reviews with customers.
6. Do NOT: promise outcomes you can't guarantee.

---

## REVIEW FOLLOW-UP (After Resolving a Complaint)

After a customer's issue has been resolved, follow up:

```
Hi [Name],

I wanted to personally follow up after our conversation on [date]. I hope
[the resolution provided] made things right. Your feedback genuinely helped us
improve, and I appreciate you giving us the chance to make it up to you.

If you feel your experience has changed, we'd be grateful if you'd consider
updating your review — but only if you feel it's warranted.

Thank you,
[Owner name]
[Business Name]
[Phone/email]
```

---

## FAKE REVIEW RESPONSE (Public — Platform)

```
We take all feedback seriously, but we have no record of this experience in
our system. If there's been a misunderstanding or case of mistaken identity,
please contact us at [phone/email] so we can investigate and help.
```

**Also:** Report the review to the platform as potentially fraudulent.

---

## HEALTH / SAFETY INCIDENT STATEMENT

For serious incidents (use in consultation with legal counsel):

```
[Business Name] takes the safety of our customers and staff as our highest
priority. We are fully cooperating with [relevant authority] and are conducting
a thorough internal review. We have [taken / are taking] the following steps:

1. [Specific action]
2. [Specific action]

We will provide updates as the situation develops. For questions, please
contact [name] at [contact info].
```
