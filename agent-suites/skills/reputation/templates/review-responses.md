# Review Response Templates by Business Type

A collection of ready-to-customize response templates organized by business type and review sentiment.

---

## RESTAURANTS & FOOD SERVICE

### 5-Star
```
Thank you so much, [Name]! We're thrilled you enjoyed [dish/experience they mentioned] —
that's exactly what our team works hard for. We'd love to have you back soon!
— [Owner/Manager name]
```

### 3-Star (Mixed)
```
Thank you for the honest feedback, [Name]. We're glad [positive thing] worked for you,
and we hear you on [complaint]. That's not the experience we aim to deliver.
We'd love a chance to do better — hope to see you again.
```

### 1-Star (Food complaint)
```
[Name], we're sorry to hear this. Food quality and consistency are everything to us,
and we clearly missed the mark. Please reach out at [phone/email] — we'd like to
make this right and understand what went wrong.
```

## HOME SERVICES (Plumbers, Electricians, HVAC, etc.)

### 5-Star
```
Thank you, [Name]! So glad [specific job] went smoothly. Our team takes pride in
leaving every home better than we found it. We appreciate you trusting us — call
anytime you need us!
```

### 3-Star
```
[Name], thanks for taking the time. We're glad we could help with [the job], and
we're sorry [issue] didn't meet your expectations. We'd like to make it right —
please give us a call at [phone].
```

### 1-Star (Complaint about service quality or pricing)
```
[Name], we're sorry this wasn't the experience you deserved. We stand behind our
work and our pricing, and we'd genuinely like to understand what went wrong.
Please call us at [phone] — we'll make it right.
```

## HEALTHCARE (Dentists, Doctors, Therapists)

### 5-Star
```
Thank you, [Name]. It means a great deal to us when patients feel heard and
well cared for. We're honored to be part of your health journey.
```

### 3-Star
```
Thank you for sharing this, [Name]. We're glad [positive experience] stood out,
and we take your feedback about [concern] seriously. Patient experience is our
priority — please contact our office at [phone] so we can discuss further.
```

### 1-Star
```
[Name], we're sorry to hear about your experience. We hold ourselves to high
standards of care and communication, and it sounds like we fell short.
Please contact our office at [phone] so we can address this personally.
```

*Note: For healthcare, avoid discussing clinical details in public responses.*

## UNIVERSAL QUICK-RESPONSE TEMPLATES

### When you don't know what the complaint is about
```
Thank you for taking the time, [Name]. We're not entirely sure what happened,
but we'd like to find out. Please contact us at [phone/email] so we can
investigate and make things right.
```

### When a reviewer is clearly wrong or a competitor
```
We take all feedback seriously. We don't have any record of this experience,
but we'd like to understand more. Please contact us at [phone/email] so we
can look into this further.
```

### When the review is very old and the issue is resolved
```
Thank you for the feedback, [Name]. We've made significant changes since this
visit — we'd love the chance to show you how much has improved. Hope to see
you again soon!
```

### When responding to a 5-star review with no text
```
Thank you for the 5 stars, [Name]! We appreciate your support — hope to see
you again soon.
```
