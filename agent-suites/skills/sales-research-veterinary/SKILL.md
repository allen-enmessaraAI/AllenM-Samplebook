## Purpose

You are the company research engine for veterinary practice prospects. Produce a complete practice intelligence brief covering 8 research dimensions — rewritten for the veterinary market.

Unlike B2B SaaS research, veterinary practice research focuses on: PIMS identification (not general tech stack), DVM count and staffing health (not employee headcount), compliance metrics (not product usage), and independence vs. corporate status (not funding stage). Every finding directly informs outreach and objection strategy.

## Phase 1: Veterinary Primary Source Research

### 1.1 Key Source Analysis

**Step 1: Google Business Profile (mandatory)**
Search "[practice name] veterinarian [city]":
- Star rating (document exact number)
- Total review count and recency (last 3 months)
- Review themes (see table below)
- Response rate to reviews
- Hours of operation: Emergency/24-hour = specialty. Evenings/weekends = high volume, client-access focused.
- Services listed in Google profile (wellness, emergency, specialty)

**Veterinary review theme detection:**
| Review Theme | What It Signals |
|-------------|-----------------|
| "Can never get an appointment" / "3-week wait" | Severe appointment backlog — capacity constraint or demand overflow |
| "They turned us away" / "Too busy to help" | Triage strain, staffing shortage |
| "Staff was rushed" / "Felt like an assembly line" | Understaffed, burnout-driven culture |
| "Prescription was expensive" / "Chewy is cheaper" | Pharmacy price sensitivity — client going online |
| "Hard to reach" / "Never called back" | Front-of-house communication failure |
| "My regular vet left" | Staff/DVM turnover — client attachment at risk |
| "Excellent Dr. [name]" with many mentions | High DVM dependency — risk if that DVM departs |

**Step 2: Practice Website (mandatory)**
Fetch the practice website. Analyze:
- Team page: Count DVMs, techs listed. Note whether specific DVMs are emphasized (dependency risk)
- Services: General companion animal only vs. exotic, large animal, specialty (surgery, dermatology, oncology, ophthalmology, cardiology, neurology). Specialty = higher revenue.
- Emergency services: After-hours, 24/7 emergency — higher revenue and complexity
- PIMS mentioned: Job postings or footer may reveal it ("Familiar with AVImark preferred")
- Online booking widget: NexHealth, ezyVet online, VetPortal, email/phone-only (friction indicator)
- In-house pharmacy mentions: Promotes in-clinic pharmacy vs. "we'll write you an Rx" (pharmacy leakage risk)
- Fear Free / Gold Standard certification: Signals investment in patient experience and staff training

**Step 3: NPI Registry Lookup (mandatory)**
Go to nppes.cms.gov. Search by practice name or DVM name.
- Taxonomy code 174M00000X = Veterinarians (general)
- Taxonomy 174MM1900X = Veterinary Dentists
- Taxonomy 174MM1800X = Veterinary Surgeons
- Confirm owner DVM name and primary address
- Multiple NPI numbers at same address = multiple DVMs in same practice

**Step 4: State Veterinary Medical Board Lookup**
Search "[state] veterinary medical board license lookup."
- Confirm owner DVM license status (Active / Suspended / Probation)
- License issue date → practice founding date proxy
- Any discipline actions noted

**Step 5: Indeed / ZipRecruiter**
Search "[practice name]" or "[practice address]" on Indeed.
| Open Role | Pain Signal |
|-----------|-------------|
| Veterinary Technician (multiple) | Severe tech shortage — workflow strain |
| Veterinary Technician (with sign-on bonus) | Desperate to hire — existing team overloaded |
| Practice Manager | Owner overwhelmed with operations, wants to step back |
| Client Service Representative | Front-of-house communication issues |
| Veterinary Receptionist | High turnover at front desk |
| Relief/Emergency DVM | Capacity overflow — can't cover all their hours |

## Phase 3: The 8 Research Dimensions (Veterinary-Specific)

### Dimension 1: Practice Overview

| Field | What to Find | How to Find It |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| Practice Name (Legal) | DBA vs. legal name | NPI + website |
| Owner DVM | Name, credentials (DVM/VMD), years at this practice | NPI + state VMB + website team page |
| Practice Type | General companion, emergency, specialty, mixed, mobile | Website + NPI taxonomy |
| Founded (est.) | Approximate age | State VMB license date |
| DVM Count | Total DVMs on staff | Team page count |
| Tech Count | CVT/RVT and unlicensed tech count | Team page |

| PIMS Identified | AVImark / Cornerstone / ezyVet / Vetspire / Other | Job posts + website + direct ask |

**Corporate affiliation identification (critical — do this first):**
- VCA: "A VCA Hospital" in branding or URL (vcahospitals.com)
- Banfield: "Banfield Pet Hospital" — inside PetSmart, always corporate
- NVA: Often retains local branding — search "[practice name] National Veterinary Associates"
- PetVet Care Centers: Search "[practice name] PetVet"
- BluePearl: Specialty/emergency chain — always corporate
- If corporate: flag as "DSO track" and note that a different sales motion is required

### Dimension 2: Revenue & Production Profile

**Revenue estimation by DVM count:**
| DVM Count | Revenue Estimate | Notes |
|-----------|-----------------|-------|
| 1 DVM, solo | $600K-$900K | Full-time owner, moderate complexity |

### Dimension 4: Team & Staffing Health

**This is uniquely important in veterinary given the staffing crisis.**

| Signal | Where to Find | What It Means |
|--------|--------------|---------------|
| Multiple open tech positions | Indeed | Understaffed — existing team is strained |
| Sign-on bonuses for techs | Indeed | Desperate to hire — serious pain |
| High team tenure (5+ year bios on website) | Team page | Stable culture, lower immediate pain |
| Recent DVM additions | Team page "Dr. [name] joined in [year]" | Growth mode |
| DVM departures visible | LinkedIn job change + reviews mentioning "my vet left" | Transition risk |
| Practice Manager listed | Team page | Owner has delegated operations — PM is your champion |
| No practice manager listed | Team page absence | Owner is sole decision-maker + operator |

**Staffing health score (internal use):**
- Green (stable): No open positions or 1-2 standard openings. Long-tenured team on website.
- Yellow (stressed): 2-3 open positions or sign-on bonuses. Some turnover signals in reviews.
- Red (crisis): 4+ open positions, multiple sign-on bonuses, reviews mentioning rushed staff.

### Dimension 5: Compliance & Retention Health

**This has no direct analog in base research — veterinary-specific.**

**Compliance rate estimation:**
Unlike dental (which has scheduling software data), compliance rates must be inferred:

| Signal | Compliance Implication |
|--------|----------------------|
| Online booking available | More likely to have systematic recall — compliance probably higher |
| Client communication platform (PetDesk) | Running reminders — compliance somewhat supported |
| Reviews mentioning "reminder" or "recall" positively | Active reminder system |
| Reviews mentioning "forgot about this" or "hadn't heard from you" | Low recall activity |
| Paper postcards mentioned (negative or dated) | Low-tech recall — compliance likely below average |
| Dedicated receptionist or CSR on team | Human-driven recall capacity |

**Pharmacy retention estimate:**
| Signal | Pharmacy Leakage Risk |
|--------|----------------------|
| Prominent in-house pharmacy page | Lower leakage — actively competing |
| "We accept pet insurance" as primary focus | May not prioritize pharmacy revenue |
| No pharmacy mentioned on website | Potential leakage to Chewy |
| Reviews mentioning prescription prices | Active client price comparison — leakage occurring |

### Dimension 6: Corporate Competitive Proximity

**Why this matters:** Independent practices in markets with VCA/Banfield/NVA within 5 miles feel competitive pressure on patient experience, hours, and technology. This pressure is your opening.

**Research approach:**
- Search "VCA [city]," "Banfield [city]," "NVA veterinary [city]," "PetVet [city]"
- Map proximity to the target practice (estimate — same zip or adjacent zip = competitive pressure)
- Corporate chains typically have 24/7 or extended hours, online booking, and multi-location brand familiarity

**Competitive proximity signals:**
| Finding | Sales Implication |
|---------|------------------|
| Corporate chain opened in same zip within 2 years | "Compete with corporate without becoming corporate" is a powerful frame |
| Practice mentions "independently owned" on website | Identity-conscious — appeals to independence message |
| Reviews comparing to "the Banfield down the street" | Client is actively comparing |

### Dimension 7: Growth Trajectory

**Positive signals:**
- New DVM added in last 12 months (team page date or LinkedIn)
- New specialty service added (social media announcement)
- New location opened
- In-house lab or ultrasound recently added (social media, website update)
- PIMS migration announced (ezyVet switch = technology investment in growth)
- Fear Free certification (culture investment)

**Stagnation/decline signals:**
- Last Google review 6+ months ago
- Team page shows fewer DVMs than reviews reference
- Multiple DVM departures visible via LinkedIn
- "Temporarily reduced hours" on website
- Practice listing on business acquisition sites (not always visible but search "[practice name] for sale")
