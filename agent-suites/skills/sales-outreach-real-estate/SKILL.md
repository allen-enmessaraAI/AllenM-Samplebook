First line must be hyper-specific. Never open with "I hope this finds you well." Lead with production language (transactions, GCI, leads, closings).
CHANNEL PRIORITY: (1) Direct email, (2) Instagram DM (agents are highly active — second only to med spas in this vertical), (3) Text message (agents respond to text faster than any other professional demographic), (4) Facebook (agent groups + personal page), (5) LinkedIn for brokerage owners and ops directors
OUTREACH TIMING: Best days: Tuesday–Thursday. Best times: 7:30–9:00am (before first showing) or 7:00–9:00pm (after last showing). Avoid: Monday AM (weekly planning/chaos), Friday PM (closing-day focus), peak spring/summer selling season (March–August) unless you have a hot trigger event.

TERMINOLOGY TO USE: GCI, transactions, closings, listing appointments, buyer consults, under contract, production, ISA, lead conversion, portal dependency, database, sphere, farm, just listed / just sold, days on market, list-to-sale ratio, kvCORE / BoomTown / Follow Up Boss / Real Geeks, Zillow Premier Agent, cap (KW), splits, team lead, closing ratio — never use generic B2B SaaS language

## Phase 1: Real Estate-Specific Personalization Research

**CRITICAL:** Real estate agents receive more cold vendor emails than almost any other professional. They have the highest pitch fatigue of any B2B vertical. The only emails that get opened are ones that demonstrate you understand their specific production level and current pain.

### 1.1 Production & Trigger Research

**Zillow profile check (3 minutes):**
Search zillow.com for the agent or team name:
- Count sold listings in last 12 months → transaction volume → GCI estimate
- Note price range → informs ROI framing (a $1M avg price agent cares about different numbers than a $250K avg price agent)
- Zillow Premier Agent badge visible? → portal spend signal
- Review count and recency: declining review velocity = losing market share

**State Real Estate Commission check (2 minutes):**
Search "[state] real estate license lookup [agent name]":
- **License date**: Under 2 years = early career agent, low budget signal
- **Brokerage listed**: If it changed in last 6 months → HOT TRIGGER — brokerage switch is the #1 most receptive moment for new tools
- **Broker vs. Salesperson**: Broker license = higher experience and decision-making authority

**Facebook Ad Library check (2 minutes):**
Go to facebook.com/ads/library and search team/agent name:
- Active buyer or seller lead gen ads → marketing spend + digital mindset
- Number of ads running simultaneously (5+ = committed spend, assess ROI)
- Ad creative quality: professional vs. DIY → sophistication signal
- No ads found → sphere/referral-focused agent OR untapped paid channel opportunity

**Instagram check (2 minutes):**
- Follower count and post frequency
- Content mix: listings vs. market updates vs. personal brand vs. educational
- Reel usage = tech-forward agent
- Business profile vs. personal profile (business = marketing-oriented)
- "Tom Ferry," "KW," or coaching-related content in bio = coachable, investing in growth

**Indeed / ZipRecruiter check (1 minute):**
Search "[team name] real estate" or "[agent name] hiring":
- "Inside Sales Agent (ISA)" posting → team is growing, lead volume outstrips conversion capacity — most powerful trigger
- "Buyer's Agent" posting → lead agent generating too many leads to handle alone
- "Transaction Coordinator" posting → closing volume is high, operational pain
- "Marketing Coordinator" → marketing budget and sophistication signal

## Phase 3: The 5-Email Real Estate Sequence

### Real Estate Email Writing Rules

**Length:** Under 80 words for emails 1–3. Under 60 words for emails 4–5. Agents are on mobile between showings.

**Voice rules for real estate:**
- Write like a knowledgeable peer who understands production, not like a vendor
- Use their language: GCI, transactions, closings, pipeline, conversion, farm, sphere
- Speak to them as a business owner first, a salesperson second
- ROI must be framed in GCI or transaction terms, never in "efficiency" abstractions
- Never say: "streamline," "leverage," "optimize," "synergize," "at scale," "robust platform"
- Never open with a question about their goals — they get that from every other vendor

**Subject lines that work in real estate:**
- "your ISA role"
- "closing ratio question"
- "what [Team] did after [X] transactions"
- "your Zillow leads"
- "re: [brokerage switch]"
- "your Q4 production"
- "noticed something in your Zillow profile"

## Phase 4: Instagram DM Strategy (Real Estate-Specific)

Instagram is the second most effective channel in real estate (after direct email) for reaching lead agents and team leaders. Many of the most productive teams are built by agents with strong Instagram followings.

**Rules:**
- Follow the agent's account first (Day 0); engage with 1-2 posts genuinely before DMing
- Wait 24-48 hours after following/engaging before sending a DM
- DM must be permission-based: "Would it be okay to send you something specific to [team name]?"
- If they respond yes: send a brief, specific value-add (a data point or relevant case study link) — not a pitch deck
- If no DM response by Day 7: pivot to email with "I sent you a DM on Instagram — not sure if it went through"

**Instagram DM sequence:**
- Day 0: Follow + leave a substantive comment on a market update or production milestone post
- Day 2: Send permission DM (see template in Email 1 section)
- Day 7: If no DM response, reference the Instagram interaction in Email 2
- Day 14: If still no response, reference Instagram comment in Email 4: "I commented on your [post] last week — wanted to close the loop here"

## Phase 5: Text Message Outreach (Unique to Real Estate)

Real estate agents carry their phone to every showing, open house, and closing. They respond to texts faster than any other professional group. Text outreach is uniquely appropriate in this vertical — NOT appropriate in dental, veterinary, or most other verticals.

**When to text:** Day 0 (before email — warm the channel) or Day 5 (if email gets no response)

**Text rules:**
- Under 160 characters (single SMS — no continuation)
- Include your first name and company name
- Reference one specific thing about them (production, trigger, or market)
- End with a closed yes/no question ("Mind if I shoot you an email?")
- Text from a real phone number — never a short code or marketing platform number

**Text templates:**
```
Day 0 (warm intro):
"Hi [name] — [your name] with [company]. Work with [X] top teams in [state].
Saw [team name] just hit [milestone/trigger]. Mind if I email you something specific?"

Day 5 (follow-up to email):
"Hi [name] — sent you an email last week re: [subject]. Worth 2 min to look at? [your name]"
```

## Phase 6: Phone Approach (Real Estate-Specific)

**When to call:** 8:30–10:00am (after morning routine, before first showing) or 5:30–7:00pm (post-showing, pre-dinner)
**Who to call:** Lead agent directly — practice managers and admins are gatekeepers but have less influence in real estate than other verticals
**Do NOT call:** During open house hours (Sat/Sun 1–4pm), during closings (agent will be unreachable), Friday afternoon in summer

**Call script:**
```
"Hi [name] — this is [your name] with [company]. Not calling to pitch you anything.
I sent you a quick note about [specific trigger or pain] and wanted to make sure
it didn't disappear into spam.

I work with [X] agent teams in [state] — including [one local reference team if possible].
[Proof point: "We helped them add 14 transactions on their existing lead database."]

I can make the case in 10 minutes. Is there a better time this week or next?"
```

**Voicemail (under 25 seconds):**
```
"Hi [name] — [your name] with [company]. Not a pitch — I noticed [specific trigger]
and have one idea that's worked for teams at your production level.
Happy to keep it to 10 minutes. My number is [phone]. Talk soon."
```
